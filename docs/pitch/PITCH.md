# PITCH — Anthrix Lost & Found

**Three-minute executive pitch**

---

## The problem (45 seconds)

Maria Santos lost her university ID badge and USB drive in the library. She found the USB only because someone posted it in a chaotic Facebook group — buried under 40 comments, impossible to search, gone in a day.

Lost-and-found at a Philippine university runs on Facebook groups, Excel sheets, and text messages. Items are found and never claimed because nobody can search them. Finders have no way to prove ownership. Losers have no way to know their item was turned in.

Facebook cannot do what we can: a persistent, searchable, claim-verified item registry with an audit trail. Posts decay. Ours doesn't.

---

## The solution (60 seconds)

Anthrix Lost & Found is a web app where anyone with an account can report a found item in four taps: category, photo, location pin, description. If the photo upload fails, the item saves as a draft — nothing is lost.

When someone loses an item, they search by category, location, and date. When they find a match, they tap "Claim," upload a proof photo (their ID badge serial, a distinguishing mark), and an admin is notified.

The admin verifies within 24 hours. Both parties get notified when the item is returned. Every step is logged — immutable, auditable, private.

Built on RA 10173 compliance: consent checkbox on every post, 90-day retention, user-triggered deletion. No health data collected.

---

## The market (30 seconds)

One real user: Maria, 22, accountancy student. She is not alone. Every campus, every small office, every barangay has the same problem — things get lost, and there is no system to get them back.

We need 25 items reunited at demo day to prove it works. Under 5 reunions in six weeks means we shut it down.

---

## The ask (45 seconds)

Anthrix operations staffs the verification queue — paid part-time, five hours a week, 24-hour turnaround. Brand assets resolved (SVG logo, favicon, feature content, contact cards). The app ships on the Anthrix Work page as a case study with the standard brand palette, theme toggle, and footer.

Demo day target: 25 items reunited in six weeks.

---

**This is not a mobile app. It is a web app that works one-handed on a cheap Android on mobile data — because that is where the users are.**
